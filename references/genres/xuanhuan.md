# Xuanhuan Genre Directing Manual (玄幻 / Immortal-Fantasy)

> Applies to: Eastern xuanhuan / immortal-fantasy / cultivation / demon-beast themed short dramas | All content is 100% original, no copyright risk
> Companion docs: take color-palette iron-rule keywords for the scene card from this chapter; the effect "color = faction" dual-discrimination axis design is in §2.4.

## 1. Genre Tone & Emotional-Curve Paradigm

### 1.1 Core Satisfier Engines

- **Playing the pig to eat the tiger (扮猪吃虎)**: the weak disguise themselves while the strong get humiliated — the audience and the protagonist share the information advantage, the antagonist does not. The pleasure comes from watching the mask come off.
- **Realm crushing (境界碾压)**: a clear hierarchy of cultivation levels (Qi Refining → Foundation Establishment → Golden Core…), and the visual contrast when a higher realm steamrolls a lower one.
- **Treasure snatching / lucky encounters (夺宝/奇遇)**: a mysterious object's arrival = the wheel of fate turning; this is the highest-frequency trigger for keyframe insertion.
- **Tribulation crossing / realm breakthrough (渡劫/破境)**: the heavens themselves respond with anomalies, a full-scene high-light moment — a naturally grand spectacle that non-fantasy genres simply do not have.
- **Identity reveal (身份揭晓)**: an ordinary mortal's origins turn out to be ancient bloodline / reincarnation, overturning what the audience thought they already knew.

### 1.2 The 90-Second Single-Episode Emotional-Curve Template (Four Beats)

| Time window | Beat | What the audience gets |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15s | Humiliation / cornered | The protagonist is suppressed (realm gap / status gap); the hook = pent-up grievance |
| 15–45s | Hidden hand / trump card | The protagonist reveals a move beyond his apparent realm (one small satisfier beat) |
| 45–75s | Breaking the deadlock / reversal | A key prop or identity surfaces and flips the situation (the high-energy point) |
| 75–90s | Crush + new suspense | The face-slap lands + a stronger enemy / higher realm appears |

### 1.3 The Multi-Episode Serial Curve

Each episode completes one "grievance → turnaround" closed loop, then opens a new thread at the end (a new enemy / new secret realm / new realm). **Satisfier density is the lifeline — at least one small satisfier every 15 seconds, and at least one major satisfier per episode.**

## 2. Worldbuilding & Visual Tone

### 2.1 Color-Palette Iron Rules for the Three Sub-Styles

| Style | Primary | Secondary | Accent | Iron rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ethereal immortal (仙气缥缈) | #DCE6F0 冷白青 (Cold White-Blue) | #A8C0D8 云青 (Cloud Cyan) / #F5F7FA 玉白 (Jade White) | #7FD4C1 灵翠 (Spirit Emerald) | High-key and clean, with cloud-mist negative space; garish colors are forbidden |
| Demon realm / dark hall (魔域/暗殿) | #1B1024 暗紫黑 (Dark Violet-Black) | #3B1F2B 暗红 (Dark Red) / #C9A227 暗金 (Dark Gold) | #E0472E 血赤 (Blood Red) | Low-key, high contrast; dark gold only for outlines and runes |
| Mortal bustle (人间烟火) | #E8D5B7 米暖 (Warm Beige) | #B87333 铜橙 (Copper Orange) / #7A5C3E 木褐 (Wood Brown) | #D94F4F 朱红 (Vermilion Red) | Warm-toned street life; lantern / candle fire as the light sources |

### 2.2 Lighting Signatures

- Immortal style: dawn-mist diffused light + spiritual-energy self-glow; shadows extremely faint, the frame clean.
- Demon realm: single-point candle / blood-array light sources, large dark areas, rim light tracing the silhouettes.
- Mortal streets: warm stove / lantern multi-point sources, soft, gentle light-dark transitions.

### 2.4 The Effect "Color = Faction" Dual-Discrimination Axis (the lifeline that prevents effects from bleeding across factions)

- Axis 1 — color: the protagonist's spiritual power is locked to **cyan/emerald** (the #7FD4C1 family); the villain's demonic energy is locked to **red/purple** (the #E0472E family). Lock the HEX values; do not change them for the entire episode.
- Axis 2 — form: even within the same color family, the forms must not be identical — protagonist = particle streams, villain = ink-like mist, supporting characters = soft halos.
- Standing negative: `禁止主角使用红色特效，禁止反派使用青色特效` (protagonist must not use red effects, villain must not use cyan effects).

## 3. Shot-Language Preferences

### 3.1 High-Frequency Shot Reference Table

| Situation | Recommended shot scale / camera move / focal length |
|---|---|
| Realm pressure | Low-angle extreme close-up (eye / jawline) + very slow push-in |
| Sword flight | Extreme wide + lateral tracking + wide-angle |
| Magic weapon / pill showcase | Extreme close-up + very slow push-in + 85mm |
| Hand-seal casting | Hand close-up + locked-off + macro feel |
| Spell clash | Two-person medium + locked-off / slight move (one primary action per shot) |
| Tribulation celestial phenomena | Extreme wide + slow crane-up + wide-angle |
| Mortal-street daily life | Medium + handheld, slight shake + 50mm |

### 3.2 Safe Writing for Martial-Action / Spell-Clash Scenes

- **Only one primary action per shot**: within the same shot, "swinging the sword" and "dodging" MUST be split into separate shots (R3).
- For spell clashes, write the "point of contact" instead of the full flight of the light orb: "two beams of light collide at the center of the frame, shockwave spreading outward" is far more stable than describing the orb's flight path.
- Body action: the sword-finger / sword-grip hand shape is the critical weak point; hand shots are always locked-off + slow.
- Long hair and robes: write "the strands / hem trail half a beat behind the motion" to create believable inertia and prevent clipping or floating-through models.

### 3.3 Pacing Control

- Dialogue scenes 6–8s per shot; spell-battle scenes 4–6s per shot; casting close-ups 4–5s per shot; celestial spectacle 8–10s per shot.
- Casting action in four beats: raise hand → form the seal → charge (spiritual energy converging) → release; about 1s per beat.

## 4. Performance & Dialogue Style

### 4.1 Action / Micro-Expression Description Templates (directly reusable)

1. Hand-seal casting: `The five fingers press together first, middle finger and thumb catching against each other; a thread of light seeps from the fingertip`.
2. Sleeve flick: `A single sweep of the wide sleeve stirs up the incense ash on the desk — the sound is small, the presence crushing`.
3. Physical reaction under pressure: `Fine sweat beads on his brow, his knees tremble slightly, jaw tight, yet he refuses to lower his head`.
4. Injury: `She wipes a thread of blood from the corner of her mouth with the back of her hand; her brow wrinkles almost imperceptibly as the knuckles brush her lips`.
5. Breakthrough moment: `Eyes closed, lashes trembling faintly; when they open again, a flash of light in the pupils comes and goes in an instant`.
6. Beast / demon nature surfacing: `In the dark, his pupils contract into a line; the corner of his mouth slowly curls up, the smile never reaching his eyes`.
7. The strong one's bearing: `He stands with hands behind his back, feet never moving from start to finish; only the hem of his robes stirs without wind`.

### 4.2 Dialogue Style (each with an original example line)

- Classical-style speech: address form + short sentence + white space — "阁下此来，是为叙旧，还是为取我的命？"
- Hard words / drawing the line: "我敬你是前辈，才容你三次。第四次，我不讲道理。"
- The identity-reveal line: "你当真以为，当年那个灭门的孤儿……是个凡人？"

## 5. High-Frequency Scenes & Props Checklist

### 5.1 High-Frequency Scenes (one-line anchors for the scene card)

1. Sect grand hall (tall pillars, spirit lamps, layered steps)
2. Immortal mountain over a sea of clouds (cliff, cloud layers, dawn mist)
3. Cave-mansion secret chamber (stone walls, sealing runes, candlelight)
4. Mortal long street (shop banners, lanterns, bluestone road)
5. Demon-realm dark hall (blood array, dark-gold ornament, ghost flames)
6. Tribulation lightning platform (sky anomalies, low-hanging thunderclouds)
7. Spirit-herb garden (spirit mist, rare flowers and plants, medicine racks)
8. Secret-realm entrance (stone formation, light screen, stele inscription)
9. Bamboo pavilion (clean bright windows, tea smoke, distant mountains)
10. Martial-arts ground (stone platform, weapon racks, a watching crowd)

### 5.2 High-Frequency Props (form weakness marked — where AI tends to draw them wrong)

| Prop | Form weakness (AI likely to mess up) |
|---|---|
| Magic weapon (sword / bell / ruler) | The direction of the engravings and the spiritual-light color (locked to a HEX value) |
| Elixir pill | Pill patterns and luster (the same pill always carries the same glow) |
| Formation | The number of array lines and the direction of rotation |
| Sound-transmission talisman / jade slip | Jade luster and the runes |
| Spirit herb | Leaf count and the glowing part |
| Universe pouch | The cloth-pouch shape + an overflowing sense of spiritual energy |
| Blood oath / covenant | Ink color and paper texture |
| Puppet / mechanism beast | The joints and mechanism engravings |
| Heavenly thunder / tribulation cloud | Lightning color (purple/white) and its shape |
| Seal stone | The crack direction and the sealing-light color |

- **Seedance**: spirit-energy particle / light-stream effects are its strength; write the effect color and form clearly (the dual-discrimination axis).
- **Kling**: ancient-costume fabric dynamics (silk / long hair) hold together relatively well; martial-action amplitude defaults low.
- **Hailuo**: ink-wash / ethereal stylization adapts well; the 6s preset suits a complete "four-beat casting" closed loop.

## 8. Original Example Excerpt — 《丹引之局》(The Elixir Gambit, abridged)

> A 90-second single-scene piece: inside a warm chamber, a life-staked probe. 12 shots, demonstrating last-frame continuation, keyframe insert · new prop (the blood pill), and keyframe insert · new character (the mysterious buyer).

### 【Scene 1】Warm Chamber Interior

**Environment**: midnight, the inner room of a warm chamber. Curtains hang low, candle flames sway; deep red, dark gold, and warm orange dominate. A thread of cold white moonlight slips through the window lattice, spreading a band of silver across the floor. The brocade couch sits on the left, a folding screen on the right; incense smoke rises straight from the burner on the desk. The carpet is dark red velvet, the candlestick tripodal.

**[00:00.0 – 00:06.0] Opening · The Blood Pill**
`[Extreme close-up | very slow push-in | a fingertip slowly spinning a blood pill]`
Yu Wan lounges on the brocade couch, red robe half slipping off her arm, a stretch of snow-white shoulder and neck showing. The gold hairpin tassel hangs down, swaying faintly with her breathing. Her fingertip slowly spins a blood-red pill bead; the blood threads inside it drift like living things. Candlelight blurs into warm bokeh in the background; the bead tints the depths of her eyes a faint red.
`[Emotion: Tension building | Hook: Visual spectacle | Connection: Jump cut (first shot of the episode — use the scene establishing image)]`

**[00:06.0 – 00:12.0] Lifting Eyes · The Falling Bead**
`[Close-up | slight push-in | the pill bead slips away, she slowly raises her eyes]`
The bead slips from between her fingers and falls into the quilt. She slowly raises her eyes — chin lifting, a curve tracing down the side of her neck, her gaze crossing the frame to land on someone. The gold earring sways; a barely-there smile touches her lips.
`[Emotion: Tension building up | Hook: Behavioral mystery | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:12.0 – 00:18.0] Entering the Game · The Opening Bid**
`[Medium | slight lateral move | Xie Lin standing by the screen, black robes, wet hair]`
Xie Lin stands at the side of the screen, long hair falling over his forehead, shoulders and back straight as if carved by a blade. He stays silent, watching the direction of the couch, gaze heavy.
`[Over-the-shoulder composition | Yu Wan as a blurred foreground]`
> **虞晚（画外音）** (voice-over): 谢公子。北山那颗头……值这个数。
> **虞晚** (after a pause, voice lower): ……考虑么。
`[Emotion: Probing | Hook: Information gap | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:18.0 – 00:24.0] Tug-of-War · Refusal**
`[Close-up | locked-off | Xie Lin's face darkens]`
Xie Lin's gaze presses downward, jaw tightening. Three seconds of silence.
`[Cut to Yu Wan | medium-close | her fingertips pick up the pill and roll it slowly over the brocade]`
> **谢凛**: ……不接。
> **虞晚** (watching the pill spin on her fingertip, a light laugh): ……哦。嫌少。
> **谢凛**: 嫌麻烦。
`[Emotion: Tug-of-war | Subtext: refusing is not about fearing the demon king — it's about fearing you | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:24.0 – 00:32.0] Rising · Closing In**
`[Medium | slow lateral move | Yu Wan sits up, the red skirt spilling off the couch edge]`
Yu Wan sits up unhurriedly, red gauze sliding from her shoulder. Her bare foot emerges from under the quilt; the toe touches the floor first, grinding a half circle on the dark red velvet rug before she stands. The red skirt trails the floor, gold embroidery flowing with each step. Clutching the pill bead, she walks toward Xie Lin step by step, stopping half a pace in front of him — tilting her head up to look at him, the curve of her neck fully exposed beneath his line of sight.
`[Two-person extreme close-up | very slow push-in | her hand holding the bead rises; the bead grazes the air past the side of his neck without touching]`
> **虞晚**: 麻烦……才值钱呢。
> **虞晚** (the bead sliding from his neck down past his jawline, stopping): 对吧？
Xie Lin's Adam's apple rolls; his gaze moves from the bead to her eyes.
`[Emotion: Tension climbing | Hook: Relationship mystery | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:32.0 – 00:40.0] Pressing Close · The Deposit**
`[Two-person extreme close-up | very slow push-in | their cheeks less than a fist apart]`
Yu Wan steps closer again. Her bare foot steps into the shadow beside his boots; the red skirt edge touches the hem of his black robe. Her hand holding the bead draws back and slowly rises to her lips — the bead presses against her own lower lip, red lips parting slightly, the blood tint making the lip color even richer. She looks up at him, her voice almost a breath.
> **虞晚**: 那……我先付定金？
The bead turns slowly by her lips; she pouts slightly, as if kissing the bead, or as if waiting for him to come take it.
`[4s silence — emotional-overload type]`
Xie Lin's breathing turns heavier for a beat; the fingers at his side twitch slightly, then he restrains them.
`[Emotion: Tension climbing | Hook: Relationship mystery | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:40.0 – 00:46.0] Submission**
`[Close-up | locked-off | Xie Lin's eyes]`
Xie Lin closes his eyes briefly. When he opens them again, all the sharpness has melted into something else.
> **谢凛**: ……听虞老板差遣。
`[Cut to Yu Wan | extreme close-up | the corner of her mouth rises slightly, having gotten her way]`
She leans in another inch, the tip of her nose almost touching his:
> **虞晚**: ……乖。
`[Emotion: Power reversal | Hook: None (emotional close) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

`[Medium | slow lateral move | Yu Wan turns her back to Xie Lin and walks to the window lattice]`
Yu Wan slowly turns, back to him, and walks to the window lattice. Cold white moonlight slants in, laying a coat of silver over her red dress. Her fingertip plays with the dust on the window lattice; her profile half-submerged in the light.
> **虞晚**: 谢公子。……今夜客栈……住满了呢。

**[00:52.0 – 00:58.0] The Key · The Fuse**
`[Extreme close-up | very slow push-in | the key turns one full circle between her fingers]`
The copper key turns one circle between her fingers, catching the moonlight, rust darkening inside the engravings. She doesn't turn around; her voice sounds like she is talking to herself, yet every word lands in Xie Lin's ears.
> **虞晚**: 二楼西边第三间……门锁，换了新的。
Behind her, Xie Lin's gaze lands on the key; his breath catches for a beat.
`[Emotion: Tension closing | Hook: Relationship mystery | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

**[00:58.0 – 01:04.0] Night Wind · A Visitor (keyframe insert · new character)**
`[Medium | locked-off | the window lattice is tapped from outside]`
Before the words fade, the window lattice is tapped lightly three times from outside — an odd rhythm, two quick and one slow. Yu Wan's finger stops on the window frame; the smile fades half a degree.
(**Keyframe insert · new character**: the visitor outside the window first appears → first generate the "window-silhouette makeup keyframe" CH-02, freeze it, then use it as the first frame of this shot.)

`[Emotion: Reversal | Hook: Identity puzzle | Connection: Keyframe insert · new character CH-02]`

---

**[01:04.0 – 01:12.0] Standoff · Three Parties**
`[Medium | slow pull-back | Yu Wan, Xie Lin, and the window silhouette share the frame]`
Yu Wan's fingertip taps back the same rhythm on the window frame — two quick, one slow. The silhouette outside freezes for an instant. She turns her head toward Xie Lin, the smile returning to her lips — but it never reaches her eyes.
> **虞晚** (softly): 谢公子……看来今晚，是谈不成了。
> **谢凛** (his gaze fixed on her face, word by word): 虞老板要的东西，我既然应了……跑不了。
`[Emotion: Crisis approaching | Hook: Three-party relationship mystery | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[01:12.0 – 01:20.0] Closing · The Flame Dies**
`[Close-up | slow push-in | the candle flame blown out by one breath of hers]`
Yu Wan turns back and breathes a soft puff toward the candle on the desk — the flame dies, the room left with only a thread of moonlight. In the dark, her voice carries no readable emotion.
> **虞晚**: 明晚，西街茶馆。带人来，带脑子来。
`[2s silence — breathing-room type]`
