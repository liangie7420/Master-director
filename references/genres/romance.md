# Romance Genre Directing Manual

> Applies to: sweet-fluff / angsty-love / tension-and-pull / marriage-first-then-love romance short dramas | All content is 100% original, no copyright risk
> Companion docs: the two-person "distance management" discipline and the safe-boundary writing for intimate/tension scenes are in §3.2 and §3.3.

## 1. Genre Tone & Emotional-Curve Paradigm

### 1.1 Core Emotional Engines

- **Tension build → probing tug-of-war → power reversal → closing**: the four-beat engine of a romance short drama; it is fundamentally a tug-of-war over "the power in the relationship."
- **Sweet point (甜点)**: the instant the relationship advances (a confession of feelings / protection / turning back).
- **Angst point (虐点)**: the instant the relationship retreats (misunderstanding / separation / sacrifice). The angst point is the amplifier of the sweet point — it makes the next sweetness land harder.
- **Ratio**: a sweet-to-angst ratio of 7:3 is recommended (the short-drama market is dominated by sweet-fluff); never let an angst beat run past 15 seconds before giving a sweet point to recover.

### 1.2 The 90-Second Single-Episode Emotional-Curve Template (Four Beats)

| Time window | Beat | What the audience gets |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | Contact point | One close-distance encounter / one look / one barbed line → the hook |
| 10–45s | Probing | Two-person tug-of-war; one relationship-advance event every 8–15s (one step closer, or one step back) |
| 45–70s | Climbing | The tension peaks (lean in — pause — unfinished action); the hook = the unresolved |
| 70–90s | Flip / close | One small confirmation OR one new misunderstanding (pick one — don't be greedy) |

### 1.3 The Tension-Climbing Ladder Method (暧昧爬坡阶梯法)

Every 8–15 seconds, one "distance event," advancing by physical distance: same-frame eye contact → over-the-shoulder → half-an-arm's distance → one-fist distance → breath-audible. **Every event must be "unfinished"** (almost touching then withdrawing, words reaching the lips then swallowed back) — the unfinished is what tension/ambiguity is made of.

### 2.2 Lighting Signatures

- Warm chamber: flickering candlelight source + window-lattice moonlight colliding; rim light on hair strands and gauze curtains.
- Sweet-fluff: large-window daylight diffused, white + light pink, exposure pushed one stop brighter.
- Angst: cold window light in rain + a warm streetlamp dot; the face half-lit, half-shadowed.

## 3. Shot-Language Preferences

### 3.1 High-Frequency Shot Reference Table

| Situation | Recommended shot scale / camera move / focal length |
|---|---|
| Tension closing in | Two-person extreme close-up + very slow push-in + 85mm |
| Heart-flutter moment | Shallow-focus close shot + locked-off + 85mm, background blurred out |
| Probing eye contact | Alternating shot/reverse close-ups + locked-off |
| Separation | Extreme wide + locked-off long take, 8–10s |
| Protection / turning back | Medium + slow pull-back + 50mm |
| Token / keepsake | Extreme close-up + very slow push-in (the keyframe-insert point) |
| Unfinished action | Close-up (hand / face) + locked-off; hold the frame 1s before cutting away |

### 3.2 Two-Person Distance Management (shot-language-ized)

- **Distance 1 — same frame**: two-person medium, both in frame with 1–2 body-widths between them → the relationship's safe zone.
- **Distance 2 — over-the-shoulder**: A over B's shoulder, B's face partly blocked by A → the relationship draws closer.
- **Distance 3 — two-person extreme close-up**: both in one extreme close-up, cheeks less than a fist apart → the tension peak.
- **Distance 4 — alternating partials**: close-ups of hands / lips / eyes alternating → maximum tension (not touching, yet more magnetic than touching).
- Descending the ladder is the mirror image: dropping from a two-person extreme close-up back to over-the-shoulder = the relationship cooling; a "half-step back" in the script must be visible in the shots.

### 3.3 Safe Boundaries for Intimate Scenes (within platform moderation + model capability)

- Use "unfinished actions" to express intimacy: lean in — hover — withdraw; lips almost touch — shift away; breath intermingles — step back.
- Angle displacement (借位): foreground blocking (gauze / a shoulder / a prop) replaces direct contact.
- Imagery substitution: candle flame / rain / petals stand in for body contact.
- Explicit boundary: contact goes no further than "cheek-to-cheek / foreheads touching / a hug"; everything else is left to white space.
- Prompt wording: write "the two faces less than a fist apart, breath intermingling, no contact" — never write the contact action itself.

### 3.4 Pacing Control

- Tension scenes 6–10s per shot (give the emotional close-up enough time); dialogue 6–8s; separation long takes 8–10s.
- "Emotional-overload silence": every tension scene needs at least one 3–4s silence shot (breathing and micro-expressions only) — it is the punctuation of a romance scene.

## 4. Performance & Dialogue Style

### 4.1 Action / Micro-Expression Description Templates (directly reusable)

1. Adam's apple: `His Adam's apple rolled once; his gaze slid off her lips and settled elsewhere`.
2. Fingertip hovering: `Her fingertip hovered above his cuff for half a second, then was gently withdrawn`.
3. Lowering the eyes: `She lowers her eyes, lashes casting a small patch of shadow; when she lifts them again, the smile has already been put away`.
4. Breathy voice: `She speaks almost entirely in a breathy voice, the tail of each word very light, as if afraid to disturb something`.
5. Earring / hair strand: `As she turns her head, the earring sways, a stray strand of hair slipping from behind her ear`.
6. Breathing change: `His breathing turned heavier for a beat, then he pressed it down; the heaviness in his chest took a long while to swallow away`.
7. Interrupting / pausing: `She bites her lower lip the moment she finishes, as if regretting speaking too fast — yet refuses to take it back`.
8. On the verge of speaking: `He opened his mouth, then finally swallowed the words back down and only nodded`.

### 4.2 Dialogue Style (each with an original example line)

- Breathy voice / white space: "……别动。"（停顿）"你头发上，有片叶子。"
- Probing double-entendre: "我请你吃饭，是看在同事三年的份上。" (Subtext: otherwise, why would I?)
- Saying one thing, meaning another: "谁等你了？我刚好路过。" (yet she hasn't moved from the spot)
- Harsh words with a hook: "从今天起，我们两清。" (yet he stands there, not leaving)
- The silence play: the fewer the lines, the more the drama — one "嗯" with three seconds of micro-expression outweighs ten sentences of explanation.

## 5. High-Frequency Scenes & Props Checklist

### 5.1 High-Frequency Scenes (one-line anchors for the scene card)

1. Warm chamber / boudoir (curtains, candlesticks, brocade couch)
2. Old-mansion courtyard (moonlight, bluestone, flowering tree)
3. City café (warm light, floor-to-ceiling windows, booths)
4. Rooftop (night wind, lights, railing)
5. Study (writing desk, candle flame, window lattice)
6. Rainy-night street (streetlamps, rain threads, headlights)
7. Studio / music room (large windows, easel, morning light)
8. Long corridor (pillar shadows, slanting sun, gauze drapes)
9. Wedding room (red candles, wedding curtains, warm light)
10. Station platform (twilight, blurred crowds, broadcast voices)

### 5.2 High-Frequency Props (form weakness marked — where AI tends to draw them wrong)

| Prop | Form weakness (AI likely to mess up) |
|---|---|
| Keepsake (jade pendant / hairpin) | The pattern and the direction of the cord knotting |
| Love letter / note | Paper text (blur) + fold creases |
| Umbrella | Rib count and canopy color |
| Ring / pair rings | Ring-band proportion and engraving (blur) |
| Photograph | Photo content (blur key parts) |
| Pocket watch / old object | Chain and case details |
| Medicine pouch / sachet | Embroidery pattern and the hanging cord |
| Wine glass | The wine color and the rim |
| Scarf / coat | Fabric texture and color (locked to a HEX value) |
| Car key / room card | Shape and brand features (avoid real brands) |

## 6. Genre-Level Failure Points & Negative Constraints

| Failure | Negative phrasing (Chinese–English) |
|---|---|
| Faces merging in two-person frames | 不要两人面部融合 do not merge faces |
| Clipping during hand contact | 不要手指穿入身体 do not clip fingers |
| Plastic-looking skin | 不要塑料皮肤 avoid plastic skin |
| Hair stuck to clothes | 不要头发与衣服粘连 avoid hair stuck to clothes |
| Stiff expressions in tension scenes | 不要僵硬表情 avoid stiff expression |
| Excessive intimacy (platform risk) | 保持距离感，二人未接触 keep distance, no physical contact |

## 7. Video-Model Adaptation Notes

- **Seedance**: facial close-ups and micro-expressions are its strength; tension-style extreme close-ups have a high success rate; breathy-line lip-sync is weak — dub it in post.
- **Kling**: two-person frames are prone to face-merging — strictly use the two reference sheets and reduce same-frame time; split into shot/reverse.
- **Hailuo**: emotional sequences (surprise first, then restraint) perform well; the 6s preset suits a single "unfinished action" closed loop.

## 8. Original Example Excerpt — 《灯下》第一集 (Under the Lamp, Episode 1)

> A 90-second single-scene piece: a winter-night bookstore, a borrowed book that starts a probing game of tension. 13 shots, demonstrating last-frame continuation, keyframe insert · new prop (the bookmark), and keyframe insert · new character (an old acquaintance).

### 【Scene 1】Old-Town Bookstore

**Environment**: winter night, an old-town bookstore. Warm yellow pendant lamps collide with the cold blue night beyond the window; between the shelves the light is dim yellow, the wooden floor worn shiny by footsteps. On the counter sit a desk lamp and a pot of tea steaming. Snow falls silently outside the door; condensation beads on the window glass.

---

**[00:00.0 – 00:05.0] Opening · A Book Under the Lamp**
`[Medium | locked-off | inside the bookstore, she sits in the window booth flipping through a book]`
Night snow lands on the window glass and melts into streaks. Shen Zhixia sits in the window booth, scarf still on, fingertip turning the pages of an old book — the pages yellowed, corners curled. Desk-lamp light falls on her face; her lashes cast a thin shadow beneath her eyes.
`[Emotion: Building | Hook: Still-life lead-in (what is she looking for) | Connection: Jump cut (first shot of the episode — use the scene establishing image)]`

---

**[00:05.0 – 00:11.0] Borrowing the Book · Eye Contact**
`[Medium | slow push-in | he appears at the end of the shelves]`
The bookstore doorbell rings once; cold air pours in. She looks up — at the end of the shelves, a young man folds his umbrella, water dripping from the tip, thin snow on the shoulders of his dark coat. His gaze crosses the shelves and lands exactly on the book in her hands.
> **陆沉** (after a pause): 那本书……借我看一眼？
`[Emotion: Building | Hook: Behavioral mystery (why this particular book) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:11.0 – 00:17.0] Passing the Book · Not Taken**
`[Close-up | locked-off | the book between them; neither lets go first]`
She closes the book and stands to hand it over. His fingers settle on the spine — she doesn't let go; he doesn't take it; the book hangs between them for two stiff seconds. She can see the snowflakes caught on his lashes.
> **沈知夏** (softly): 借可以。但别弄脏。
> **陆沉** (his gaze moving from the book to her face): ……不会。
`[Emotion: Probing | Hook: Relationship mystery (the two seem to know each other) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:17.0 – 00:23.0] Old Acquaintance · Recognition (keyframe insert · new character, finalized state)**
`[Medium | slow push-in | she recognizes him; the smile withdraws for an instant]`
She suddenly recognizes him — her pupils dilate slightly, the smile withdraws for a beat, then returns to normal so fast it's almost invisible. He, however, doesn't miss that glance; the corner of his mouth moves almost imperceptibly.
> **沈知夏**: ……是你啊。
> **陆沉**: 是我。七年没见，你还在看这本书。
`[Emotion: Relationship surfacing | Hook: Information gap (what happened seven years ago) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:23.0 – 00:29.0] The Bookmark · Falling (new prop)**
`[Extreme close-up | very slow push-in | an old bookmark slips out of the book]`
He opens the book; an old bookmark floats out from between the pages — the paper yellowed, a faded pen-written character at the edge. Seeing the writing, her breath catches half a beat; she reaches to catch it, her fingertip stopping one inch above the bookmark.
(**Keyframe insert · new prop**: the old bookmark debuts → first generate the "bookmark close-up establishing frame" PR-01, freeze it, then use it as the first frame of this shot.)
> **沈知夏** (voice tightening): ……这书签，不是我的。
> **陆沉** (looking at her eyes): 我知道。七年前，我放的。
`[Emotion: Emotional impact | Hook: Information gap (what is written on the bookmark) | Connection: Keyframe insert · new prop PR-01]`

---

**[00:29.0 – 00:35.0] Night Tea · Seating Close**
`[Medium | slow lateral move | he sits in the booth across from her]`
He pulls out the chair across from her and sits; the book is placed at the corner of the table between them — not pushed toward her, not pulled back. The kettle on the counter steams; vapor floats in the lamplight.
> **陆沉**: 七年了。这本书，你看完没？
> **沈知夏** (lowering her eyes): 没看完。中间断了好几年。
`[Emotion: Tug-of-war | Hook: Information gap | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:35.0 – 00:43.0] Palm Prints · Covering**
`[Close-up | locked-off | both hands land on the book at opposite ends]`
They reach for the book at the same time — his palm covers the spine, her fingertips land on the cover; across the book's width their hands almost overlap, but never touch. He glances at her hand and doesn't move his away.
`[3s silence — probing-standoff type]`
> **陆沉** (low voice): 当年我走的时候，欠你一句解释。
`[Emotion: Tension climbing | Hook: Information gap (the explanation from back then) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:43.0 – 00:51.0] Closing In · Unfinished**
`[Two-person extreme close-up | very slow push-in | across the table, their cheeks drawing closer]`
She lifts her eyes to him; he leans in — across the table, their faces less than a fist apart, breath almost intermingling. Her lashes tremble; she doesn't pull away. His gaze rests on her lips, pauses, and goes no further.
> **沈知夏** (breathy): ……解释，我等你七年。不差这一晚。
`[Emotion: Tension peak | Hook: Unfinished action (will he kiss down) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:51.0 – 00:57.0] Withdrawing · Stepping Back**
`[Close-up | slight pull-back | he slowly sits back]`
He looks at her for a long while, then slowly settles back into his seat, rubbing a fingertip across the book's spine — as if swallowing back whatever he was about to say.
> **陆沉**: ……现在还不是时候。等我把一件事处理完，再来还你一个解释。
`[Emotion: Stepping back | Hook: Information gap (what does he need to handle) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[00:57.0 – 01:03.0] Phone Call · Sudden Shift (keyframe insert · new character)**
`[Medium | locked-off | his phone rings; he glances at it, expression shifting slightly]`
His phone vibrates on the table; the screen lights up — he glances at the caller ID, his expression changes almost imperceptibly, and when he answers his voice is pressed very low.
(**Keyframe insert · new character**: the person on the other end never appears on screen; the "incoming-call screen close-up" PR-02 serves as the keyframe hinting at their arrival.)
> **陆沉** (low): ……知道了。我现在过去。
`[Emotion: Crisis interruption | Hook: Information gap (what the call said) | Connection: Keyframe insert · new prop PR-02]`

---

**[01:03.0 – 01:09.0] Leaving the Book · Parting Words**
`[Medium | slow pull-back | he rises, the book left on the table]`
He stands, leaving the old book on the table; before he goes, he glances at her, his gaze pausing on the bookmark's spot for a moment.
> **陆沉**: 书你留着。书签……你看了，就明白了。
> **沈知夏** (not stopping him, fingertip resting on the book): ……一路平安。
`[Emotion: Parting | Hook: The bookmark mystery | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[01:09.0 – 01:15.0] Doorbell · Monologue**
`[Medium | locked-off | she watches him walk into the snow]`
The doorbell rings once; he opens his umbrella and walks into the snow, his back receding under the streetlamp. She looks down at the book; her fingertip traces the faded writing on the bookmark — words she wrote seven years ago, a sentence never finished.
> **沈知夏** (softly, word by word): "等我回来——"（停顿）"……我回来过了。"
`[Emotion: Emotional close | Hook: Identity puzzle (he came back seven years ago?) | Connection: Last-frame continuation]`

---

**[01:15.0 – 01:20.0] Closing · The Snow Stops**
`[Extreme wide | locked-off long take | beyond the bookstore window, the snow slowly stops]`
Outside the window, the snow slowly stops. The bookstore's lights are still on; she sits alone by the window, closes the old book, and holds it to her chest. In the distance under a streetlamp, a figure with an umbrella stands for a long time before turning and walking away.
`[3s silence — breathing-room type]`
`[Emotion: Episode-ending hook | Hook: Behavioral mystery (why did he stand in the snow) | Connection: Last-frame continuation (Episode 2's first shot)]`
